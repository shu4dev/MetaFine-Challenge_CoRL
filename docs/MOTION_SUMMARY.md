# Motion summary of the CoRL demos

Generated by `python tools/motion_summary.py --demos-root demos/CoRL --tasks toggle_switch_table grasp_move_mug --window 50 --stride 10 --fs 20 --closed-thresh 0.02 --kw-unit episode --subset train --split-json data/split_v1.json` from the `action` and `observation.state` columns of the LeRobot frame tables (no videos).

Tables and tests use the train episodes of `data/split_v1.json` (whole-episode split, see tools/build_index.py).

Settings: window 50 steps (2.50 s), stride 10, fs 20 Hz (dt 0.050 s; the fps label in info.json is not used), gripper closed when the mean of the two finger joints is below 0.02 m, window stage by majority of its frames.

Metrics per window over the 7 arm-joint columns of `action` (pd_joint_delta_pos, normalized joint deltas with 1.0 = 0.1 rad), cells show mean (median) over windows:

- action change: mean |a[t+1] - a[t]| (first difference)
- roughness: mean |a[t+2] - 2 a[t+1] + a[t]| (second difference, the proxy behind the "2.4x smoother" number)
- band fractions: share of the rFFT energy of the raw window in 0-1, 1-3, 3-6 and 6-10 Hz, per joint then averaged over the joints with energy; the DC bin counts towards 0-1 Hz, so a window of steady motion scores close to 1 there

Stages: "open" = approach / retreat (fingers apart), "closed" = transport / contact (fingers together), "all" = every window of the variant.

## Notes

- Windows overlap (stride smaller than the window) and several windows come from one episode, so window-level tests overstate significance; the p-values above use one value per episode unless `--kw-unit window` was given.
- The stage proxy is the gripper only: a task whose gripper never closes (or never opens) has one empty stage row, and the across-stage test for that variant is n/a. Verify on the real files that the finger joints read about 0.04 m when open and near 0 when closed.
- Kruskal-Wallis compares distributions, not means; a small p-value with similar medians means a shape difference.
- `data/motion_summary.csv` holds every window (task_id, episode_index, variant, instruction, split, start, end, stage, closed_frac, mean_abs_action, action_change, mad, roughness, frac_0_1, frac_1_3, frac_3_6, frac_6_10) for other cuts; `mad` is the mean L2 norm of the first difference over dt (`utils/util.py::compute_mad`), `split` is train / val from the split file or none.
